Old Money Mood

Social Publisher

A private automation tool that publishes pre-produced short lifestyle videos to the Old Money Mood accounts on Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok on a fixed schedule.

What it does

The owner uploads finished videos to a media store. A scheduled job then posts them, a few per day, to the owner's own connected social accounts using each platform's official content-publishing API. There is no user-facing app, sign-up, or feed — only the account owner interacts with it.

Data

It stores only what is needed to publish: an access token for each of the owner's own social accounts, and the queue of videos and captions to post. It does not collect data from any other person and has no other users. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
